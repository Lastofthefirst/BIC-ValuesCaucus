## DPI/NGO conference:

**September 15, 1999** — "Transforming Society: Integrating Ethics and Values into Action Plans" organized by members of the Values Caucus Jerry Spivack, Martha Gallahue and Patrick McNamara.

## Events sponsored:

**November 4, 1999** – "Spiritual Values in Foreign Policy and Sustainable Development," a farewell address by Alfredo Sfeir-Younis, hosted by H.E. Mr. S.R. Insanally of Guyana

**November 3, 1999** – Reception in honor of Alfredo Sfeir-Younis, Representative of the World Bank to the United Nations.

## Events co-sponsored:

**November 5, 1999** – Sixth Annual Day for Freedom of Religion or Belief with the NGO Committee on Freedom of Religion or Belief, Committee of Religious NGOs at the UN, NGO Committee on Human Rights

**September 23, 1999** – A Multi-Religious Prayer Service with the Committee of Religious NGOs

**September 16, 1999** – An Interfaith Celebration of Commitment to the Work of the UN with the Interfaith Center of New York and numerous NGOs

**September 14, 1999** – International Day of Peace observance at the UN and worldwide with the Department of Public Information and numerous NGOs

## Projects:

- NGO Code of Ethics – ongoing project and discussion group
- Disarmament Declaration
- Millennium Forum – values thematic area
